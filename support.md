## Common Questions

**How do I connect my Planning Center account?**
Go to Settings in the app and tap "Connect Planning Center." You'll be guided through a secure sign-in process.

**How do I restore my subscription?**
Go to Settings and tap "Restore Purchases." Your subscription is tied to your Apple ID and will restore automatically.

**Where is my data stored?**
All your songs, click sets, and arrangements are stored locally on your device. We do not store any data on external servers.

**How do I export a click set?**
Tap the share icon on any click set to export it as a `.clickset` file. You can share it via AirDrop, Messages, or any other method.
